    Method and limitations
    
      
      
        
    • Nested CV, no leakage. Outer 5-fold CV on the 40 training rows evaluates each (scheme, k). Inside every outer-training fold the scheme selects features (and Lasso / Elastic Net tune their penalties) with an inner 5-fold CV; the chosen subset is refit with plain OLS and scored on the untouched outer fold. Scaling is fitted inside the same folds.
      • 
        
    • The CV score rates the procedure, not one subset. Different outer folds may pick different subsets, so two schemes whose final subset is the same can still have different CV RMSE. "Fold agreement" shows how many outer folds picked the final subset.
      • 
        
    • RFE vs RFECV. RFE removes the weakest feature (smallest standardized OLS coefficient) until exactly k remain, so it fits the k = 1–5 grid. RFECV instead chooses k itself by CV; it is shown only as a reference: .
      • 
        
    • Lasso / Elastic Net top-k. These rank features by |coefficient| of the tuned penalized model and keep the top k, then refit OLS. The penalized coefficients are only a ranking; when the penalty sets several coefficients to zero, the order among those zeros is a tie, broken by column order.
      • 
        
    • Small data. 50 startups, 40 for training and 10 for testing. CV differences of a few hundred dollars are within fold-to-fold noise, and test metrics from 10 rows are unstable — which is exactly why the test set is not used for choosing k.
