Suggested Assignment: CRISP-DM + Feature Selection
For any selected dataset, students can complete:
1. Business Understanding: Define the continuous prediction target.
2. Data Understanding: Verify sample size, feature count, missing values and correlations.
3. Data Preparation: Train/test split, missing-value handling and categorical encoding.
4. Modeling: Train LinearRegression , Ridge and Lasso.

==== [SRC-2 p.4] ====
5. Evaluation: Compare MAE, MSE, RMSE, R² and Adjusted R²; check the five regression assumptions.
6. Deployment: Predict new samples and explain the model.
For Feature Selection, compare SelectKBest, RFE, Sequential Feature Selection, Lasso and permutation importance, selecting 14, 20, 25 or 30 features using training data only.
My first choice is Predict the Flood, because its 20 numerical features meet your constraint directly and avoid the extra complexity of categorical encoding.
